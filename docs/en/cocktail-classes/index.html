Cocktail class in Chemnitz

Learn to mix cocktails where they're usually mixed for you. You stand behind our counter, learn to shake, measure and balance sweet against sour, and then drink what you've made. For birthdays, hen and stag parties and team events.

Enquire about a class Call +49 15562 688753

01The class

Two and a half hours behind the bar

No lecture, no slides. Our bartender makes each drink once, step by step, and explains what matters. Then everyone takes a turn at the shaker while the rest of the group sits at the counter, watches and drinks along.

Our bartender picks the three drinks: three of our own that build on each other, from simple to more demanding. We use what always goes into our drinks: fresh limes, real fruit purées, homemade syrups. If you don't drink alcohol, you mix the same drinks as mocktails, for the same price.

“By the end of the class everyone knows why their daiquiri used to be too sweet.”

04Questions

Before you ask

When do classes take place?

On Mondays and Tuesdays, our closing days. Classes usually start between 5 and 7 pm, we agree the exact time with you.

Do we need any experience?

No. The class starts from zero and is still fun if someone already mixes at home.

Can we do it without alcohol?

Yes. Every drink works as a mocktail, the price is the same. Let us know beforehand who is mixing alcohol-free.

Can we come as two?

Yes. The regular class at €59 per person is for 6 or more, but we also run it from 2. There's no list price for that, we send an individual quote, because the bartender, ingredients and set-up are shared by fewer people. Tell us how many you are and we'll give you a fixed price in advance.

How does the class work?

Our bartender makes each drink once from start to finish and explains the steps. Then everyone takes a turn. Three drinks per person.

Can we choose the drinks?

The bartender chooses: three of our own drinks that build on each other. That's deliberate, you learn more in two and a half hours than if the same idea ends up in the glass three times.

If someone really dislikes something or can't have it, tell us in your enquiry and we'll take it into account.

Is there food?

Yes, at no extra cost: sandwiches and a snack platter. If you're planning more, you can order catering or bring something along.
