Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The German Datenschutzerklärung is legally binding.

1. Privacy at a glance

The following notes give a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you visit this website. Personal data is any data that can be used to identify you personally. This website is deliberately lean: there is no contact form that sends data to us, no newsletter, no cookies and no analytics or tracking services.

3. Hosting and server log files

When you visit this website, the hosting provider automatically collects information in server log files that your browser transmits automatically. This includes browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, host name of the accessing computer, time of the request and IP address. This data is not combined with other data sources. It is collected on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The website operator has a legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and optimisation of the website, for which the server log files must be recorded.

4. Storage in your browser (session storage)

On your first visit this website shows a short opening animation. So that it doesn't appear again on every page, the entry “kb-intro” is saved in your browser's session storage. It contains no personal data, is not transmitted to us or to third parties and is deleted automatically when you close the browser window. No cookies are set.

5. Fonts (hosted locally)

This site uses web fonts for consistent display. The font files are stored on the server of this website and delivered from there. No connection to Google or other third-party servers is made and no data is passed on to third parties.

6. Links to external services

This website contains links to external services, such as our Instagram profile, Google Maps (directions) and our drinks menu as a PDF. These are simple links, not embedded content. Only when you click such a link do you leave this website, and the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

7. Contacting us

If you contact us by phone, email or Instagram, your details are stored by us to handle your enquiry and in case of follow-up questions. The enquiry window on this website does not transmit any data itself, it only opens your own email program. We do not pass this data on without your consent. Processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to entering into or performing a contract (for example a reservation), and otherwise on our legitimate interest in handling enquiries effectively (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

8. Your rights

Within the applicable legal provisions you have the right at any time to free information about your stored personal data, its origin and recipients and the purpose of processing, as well as the right to have this data corrected, blocked or deleted. You also have the right to restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR), to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) and to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR). You can contact us at any time about this or any other questions about personal data.

9. Right to lodge a complaint

In the event of a breach of the GDPR, data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of their habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged breach. For Saxony this is the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner: www.datenschutz.sachsen.de.

10. SSL/TLS encryption

For security reasons and to protect the transmission of confidential content, this site uses SSL/TLS encryption when accessed via https://. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the padlock symbol in your browser's address bar.
