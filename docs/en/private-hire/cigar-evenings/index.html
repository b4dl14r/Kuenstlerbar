Cigar evening in Chemnitz

A good cigar needs time, the right drink and company that's in no hurry. That's why we open Künstlerbar for private groups on Mondays and Tuesdays. Guests aged 18 and over only.

Enquire about a cigar evening Call +49 15562 688753

01The evening

A cigar and the right glass

Each cigar comes with two drinks to match: usually a strong rum or whisky served neat, and a mixed drink that picks up the sweetness without covering it. If you'd rather stick to cocktails, you can choose from our menu.

There's no fixed programme. Our bartender stays with your group, explains what's in the glass if you want to know, and otherwise keeps in the background.

On every other evening smoking is on the terrace and inside is non-smoking. The private cigar evening is the exception. Mention it in your enquiry and we'll work out together how to run the evening.

Six to twenty guests, 18 and over.

02Included

What's included

One cigar
From a small selection, from mild to full-bodied. We provide cutter and light.
Two drinks
Chosen to match: one neat, one mixed. Alcohol-free on request.
Snacks
Sandwiches and a snack platter at no extra cost. Rum and cigars call for something solid.
Private group
The bar belongs to your group alone that evening.
18 and over
Adults only. We check, politely but reliably.
