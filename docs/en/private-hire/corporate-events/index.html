Corporate events and team nights in Chemnitz

Christmas party, team night, client reception or a drink after the workshop: on Mondays and Tuesdays Künstlerbar belongs to your company. Your own bar team, no other guests, right in the city centre.

01Why here

Small team, big night

Large function rooms are the wrong setting for small teams: too much space, not enough atmosphere. Here you sit close enough for conversations to happen, with a proper bar behind you. It works for ten people as well as for sixty.

We set the room up the way you need it: a long table for the Christmas party, open space for a reception, or everything as it is. If you like, the evening starts with a tasting or a cocktail class, and the bar carries on as normal afterwards.

Ten to sixty guests, exclusive.
