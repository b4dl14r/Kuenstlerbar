02Formats

How the evening should go

Everything can be combined, for example a tasting to start and an open bar afterwards. Each page has the full details.

  • Corporate event Christmas party, team night, client reception. €299 plus drinks
  • Cocktail class You get behind the counter yourselves. €59 per person
  • Tasting Five drinks, explained and tasted. €49 per person
  • Cigar evening Cigars and matching drinks, 18+. €59 per person

All prices include VAT. The €299 room hire is a flat rate for the evening, whatever the number of guests. Programme prices are per person and include the drinks listed plus sandwiches and a snack platter. Any other drinks are charged as you go, at the prices on the drinks menu.

03The room

Small enough to keep it among yourselves

Dark wood, warm light, the curved counter and the lit bottle shelf behind it. A group fills the room without getting lost in it, and it's small enough that by the end of the night everyone has talked to everyone.

We set the room up the way you need it: one long table, open space for standing, or everything as it is. Music runs through our sound system and we're happy to play your playlist.

04Questions and answers

What people usually ask

If something is missing, just ask us.

When can we hire the bar?

On Mondays and Tuesdays. The bar is closed to the public on those days and belongs entirely to your group. On all other days the bar is open as usual. We're happy to reserve seats for larger groups then, but it won't be exclusive.

How many people fit in?

Up to about 60, spread across tables, the seating area and the counter. From around 45 guests it becomes a standing reception with some seating, below that everyone gets a seat. Tastings and cocktail classes work best with smaller groups so everyone fits at the counter.

What does it cost?

Room hire is €299 for the evening, including VAT, whether there are twelve of you or sixty. Drinks are charged as you go, at our normal menu prices. A programme costs extra per person: tasting €49, cocktail class €59, cigar evening €59. All prices include VAT and a snack platter.

We don't ask for a minimum spend. Nobody can estimate that reliably in advance, and you shouldn't spend the evening doing sums.

Can we have food?

Every booked programme comes with sandwiches and a snack platter at no extra cost.

We don't have our own kitchen. If you want more, you're welcome to order catering or bring finger food. Let us know in your enquiry and we'll set up tables and plates.

How far ahead do we need to book?

At least two weeks before your preferred date. Buying, staff planning and preparation need that time, so the enquiry form only lets you pick dates at least two weeks ahead.

Mondays and Tuesdays between early November and New Year go quickly. For Christmas parties, ask in September or October.

Can we do a cocktail class for two?

Yes. The regular cocktail class at €59 per person is for 6 or more, but we also run it from 2 people. There's no list price for that, we send you an individual quote, because the bartender, ingredients and set-up are shared by fewer people. Tell us how many you are and we'll give you a fixed price in advance.

How long does an evening last?

Usually it starts between 5 and 7 pm and ends around midnight. If you'd like to stay longer, mention it in your enquiry. That can usually be arranged.

Do you need a deposit?

Yes, for every booking. The €299 room hire is due as a deposit when we confirm. Only then is the date fixed for you.

It doesn't cost anything extra: the deposit is deducted from your bill at the end of the evening. Payment details come with the booking confirmation.

Can we play our own music?

Yes, your playlist runs through our sound system. The room isn't really made for a DJ setup. If you're planning something bigger, talk to us first.

Is the bar accessible?

Access is not step-free. Let us know if someone in your group has limited mobility. We'll tell you honestly what works and usually find a solution.

Can we smoke?

Smoking is on the terrace, inside is non-smoking. The only exception are our cigar evenings for private groups.

Tell us about your evening

Date, number of guests, occasion. That's all we need to tell you if it works.
