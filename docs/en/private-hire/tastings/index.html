Cocktail tasting in Chemnitz

Five drinks in small glasses, one after the other, with someone behind the counter explaining why they taste the way they do. For private groups and companies, on Mondays and Tuesdays with the bar to yourselves.

Enquire about a tasting Call +49 15562 688753

01How it works

Five drinks, no rush

We start with something clear and fresh, work our way through sours and fruitier drinks and finish with something strong. Each drink is a small pour. Nobody ends up drinking too much, but everyone has tried five very different drinks.

Along the way we tell you what's in the glass: why fresh lime and bottled juice make two different drinks, what a syrup does, how much ice is right. Questions are very welcome, that's the difference to a round at the bar.

“The best moment is when someone finds a new favourite they would never have ordered.”

From Künstlerbar
