01The bar

Our living room on Hartmannstraße

Dark wood, warm light and a counter you won't want to leave in a hurry. Meet friends, get talking to strangers, and there is nearly always a seat free.

We don't take shortcuts with the drinks. Limes are squeezed fresh, syrups are made in house, fruit goes into the glass as real purée. That goes for the espresso martini as much as for our own drinks like the Tropical Grind.

“A bar is just a room with a counter. What it turns into is up to the people who walk in.”

Julian Hahnen, owner

02Drinks

What goes in the glass

Cocktails, mocktails and lemonades are made by hand. If you'd rather have a beer, a glass of wine or a cola, that's fine too.

Every drink on the menu lists what's in it and what it costs. If we run out of something, it comes off the menu.

03Mondays and Tuesdays

The whole bar, just for you

On our closing days, Künstlerbar belongs to your group. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll plan the rest with you.

  • Hire the bar The whole bar and our bar team, nobody else in the room. €299 room hire
  • Corporate event Christmas party, team night or client reception. €299 plus drinks
  • Cocktail class Get behind the counter and drink what you mix. €59 per person
  • Tasting Five drinks, one after the other, explained as you go. €49 per person
  • Cigar evening A good cigar, two drinks to match, plenty of time. €59 per person

All prices include VAT. Room hire is a flat rate for the evening, programme prices are per person and include sandwiches and a snack platter. Any other drinks are charged as you go, at the prices on the drinks menu.
